Test Scenarios

Weight and count inside limits; baseline pricing response.

Minimal weight and count inside limits; client delivery cost stayed $0.

Product Weight Exceeding Maximum Limit

Count inside limits, weight 6.01 kg (over the 6 kg cap).

Product Count Exceeding Maximum Limit

Weight inside limits, product count one over the max.

Bug: excess client delivery cost was not applied.

Maximum Weight Within Limits

Weight at the 6.00 kg cap with count inside limits; pricing matched spec.

Inconclusive Tests

Two tests had no clear expected price because the requirements leave a gap in host delivery tiers.

Example case:

Ordering Fast Delivery with Product Weight Slightly Below the Upper Limit (2.59 kg).

Testing Results

Click image to enlarge

Major Design Flaw

Requirements list $3 for 0–2.5 kg and $6 for 2.6–6 kg. Weights between those bands (e.g. 2.51 kg and 2.59 kg) have no stated host price, so expected results for fast delivery at 2.59 kg were unclear.

Conclusion

Open items from the run:

  • Kit item limit checks differ for duplicate vs mixed product IDs
  • Invalid product IDs return 200
  • Delivery pricing spec has a gap between weight tiers; two cases stayed inconclusive
